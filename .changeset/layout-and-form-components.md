Add layout, navigation, and form components.

- `Accordion` and `Collapsible` organize expandable sections with Base UI keyboard and ARIA handling.
- `Progress` and `Slider` cover determinate progress and single or range values.
- `Sheet` provides edge-anchored panels on top of the Dialog focus trap and dismissal behavior.
- `Toggle Group` and `OTP Input` add segmented controls and one-time-code entry.
- `Breadcrumb`, `Kbd`, `Visually Hidden`, and `Aspect Ratio` round out navigation and layout helpers.
